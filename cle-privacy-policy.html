Privacy Policy

CLE Complete 2026 · Effective August 2, 2026

Overview

CLE Complete 2026 ("CLE Complete", "the app", "we", or "us") is an independent educational study tool. The app does not require an account and we do not operate a server that receives your answers or study history.

Information stored on your device

The app stores study progress, quiz answers, scores, bookmarks, preferences, notification settings, Study Points, pet progress, and unlocked themes in its local database. This information is used to provide review, progress, and personalization features. The app's database is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer rules. You can remove local information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch a rewarded ad; core study features remain available without watching one. Google and its advertising partners may process device and app information, IP address, advertising identifiers, consent choices, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where permitted. These activities are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required, the app requests advertising consent before requesting ads and provides an in-app entry point for available ad privacy choices.

Google Play purchases

Optional permanent cosmetic theme and pet collections are processed by Google Play. Payment credentials are handled by Google, not by CLE Complete. The app receives product and purchase-status information needed to unlock or restore a purchase. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Daily study reminders are optional. If enabled, the app requests Android notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. You can disable reminders in the app or Android system settings.

Sharing and selling information

We do not sell your study history or personal information. Information may be processed by Google only as described above when you use optional ads or Google Play purchase services.

Security and retention

Study data is retained locally until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. No method of storage is completely risk-free, but the app limits its own data handling to what is needed for its features.

Children

CLE Complete is intended for licensure-examination candidates and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • Do not watch optional rewarded advertisements.
  • Use the app's Ad privacy options when available.
  • Disable notifications in the app or Android settings.
  • Clear local data through Android settings or uninstall the app.

Independent status

CLE Complete is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Professional Regulation Commission, the Board of Criminology, or any government entity. Questions are original and based on publicly available syllabi. The app does not guarantee examination results.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to clecomplete.app@gmail.com.
