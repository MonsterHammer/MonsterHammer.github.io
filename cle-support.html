Help, feedback, privacy, purchases, and independent-app information.

Contact

Email clecomplete.app@gmail.com for support, bug reports, purchase-entitlement issues, or privacy questions. Please do not include passwords, full payment information, purchase tokens, or other sensitive personal data.

Common troubleshooting

Install the latest Google Play version and restart your device. If a problem continues, include the app version, Android version, and a short description of what happened.

Rewarded ads are optional and depend on internet access, consent status, region, and ad inventory. A reward is granted only after Google reports completion.

Purchases and restoration

Theme and pet collections are non-consumable one-time products processed by Google Play. Use the app's Restore purchases option while signed into the same Google Play account used for the purchase. Google Play controls receipts, cancellations, and refunds.

If Google Play shows a completed purchase but the collection is not unlocked after restoring, email support with the product name and Google Play order ID. Do not send card or bank details.

Data reset

Use Settings & About → Reset all progress to erase local study records, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. The app does not create a CLE Complete account.
