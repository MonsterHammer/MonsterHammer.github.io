Common troubleshooting

If the app does not open correctly, install the latest Google Play version and restart your device. If study data appears incorrect, include the app version and Android version in your message.

Rewarded ads are optional and depend on internet access, consent status, and ad availability. A reward is granted only after Google reports that the ad was completed.

Purchases

Theme and pet collection purchases are processed by Google Play. Use the app's Restore purchases option while signed into the same Google Play account used for the purchase.
